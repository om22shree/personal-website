## Which DevOps certificates have actual market worth
----------------------------------------
#### less than 3 years of experience
- CKAD (Kubernetes Application Developer - Linux Foundation --- good to have)
- AWS SAA (solutions architect associate --- good to have)

#### more than 4 years of experience
- CKA (Kubernetes Administrator - Linux Foundation --- good to have)
- AWS SAP (solutions architect professional --- good to have)
- CKS (Kubernetes security speciality - Linux Foundation --- optional)

#### Note: certificates by themselves do not make you a DevOps engineer. You need to have a solid understanding of the tools and technologies you are using. On top of this, you need to have projects and demonstrated experience in using these tools and technologies.

#### You can get more specialized certifications if you want but the above mentioned certifications are the most valuable ones in the market right now
